Our story

A backyard hive became CloverBee.

CloverBee is built around the part of beekeeping we like most: being close enough to the hives to notice what is happening, care for them properly, and appreciate where every jar starts.

The website uses our own apiary photos rather than stock images because the place, the hives, and the bees are part of the product.

Real bees. Real backyard. Real honey.
