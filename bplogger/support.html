BP Logger

Support

BP Logger is a simple blood-pressure logging app for recording systolic and diastolic readings, reviewing history, exporting CSV files, and optionally saving readings to Apple Health or Android Health Connect.

Getting help

For support, bug reports, or questions about BP Logger, email developer@cloverbee.ca.

Apple Health permissions

If BP Logger cannot save readings to Apple Health, confirm that Health access is enabled for BP Logger in your iPhone settings. You can continue saving readings locally even when Health access is unavailable.

Android Health Connect permissions

If BP Logger cannot save readings to Health Connect, confirm that BP Logger has permission to write blood-pressure data in Health Connect settings. Local logging remains available without Health Connect access.

CSV export

Use the export option in BP Logger to create a CSV file containing your saved readings. The file can then be shared or saved using your device's standard share sheet.

Medical use

BP Logger is intended for personal record keeping only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
