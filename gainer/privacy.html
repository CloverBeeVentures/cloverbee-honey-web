Gainer

Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

In short: Gainer is designed as a local-first workout logger. Your workout data is stored on your device. The current version does not require an account, upload workout data to CloverBee servers, use advertising, or use analytics or tracking services.

Information Gainer handles

Gainer lets you create exercises and routines and record workout information such as exercise names, sets, repetitions, weight, notes, equipment labels, workout dates, and workout duration.

Local data storage

Workout data is stored in Gainer's local database on your device. The current version does not require a CloverBee account and does not synchronize your workout history with a CloverBee cloud service.

Health platforms

The current version of Gainer does not read from or write workout information to Apple Health, HealthKit, Android Health Connect, or another health platform.

If optional health-platform integration is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that feature is released where required. Local Gainer storage will remain authoritative unless the product's disclosed behaviour changes.

Media controls

On supported Android devices, Gainer can interact with the device's active media session to display and control currently playing media during a workout. Gainer does not upload media information to CloverBee servers. Media-control availability depends on the device, operating system, permissions, and media app.

The current iOS version does not provide equivalent media-control integration.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of Gainer does not contain third-party advertising and does not use analytics, tracking, or profiling services.

Data sharing

Gainer does not sell your workout data. The current version does not transmit your workout history to CloverBee or to third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics.

Permissions and your choices

Some platform features may require operating-system permissions. You can control those permissions through your device settings. Core workout logging remains local to the app.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes its local app data according to the operating system's normal app-removal behaviour. Where Gainer provides an in-app delete or edit function, those changes apply to the local Gainer database.

Children

Gainer is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not operate an account or cloud service that collects user workout data in the current version.

Changes to this policy

If Gainer's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released where required.

Contact

Questions about Gainer privacy can be sent to developer@cloverbee.ca.
