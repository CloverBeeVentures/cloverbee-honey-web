Gainer

Support

Gainer is a local-first strength-training logger for building routines, recording sets, repetitions and weight, keeping workout history, and carrying previous performance into your next workout.

Getting help

For support, bug reports, or questions about Gainer, email developer@cloverbee.ca.

Workout data

Gainer stores workout information locally on your device. If you are troubleshooting a problem, do not uninstall the app unless you are prepared to lose locally stored Gainer data.

Active workout recovery

If you leave Gainer during a workout, the active workout is saved locally and can be resumed from the home screen. If Resume Workout does not appear after reopening the app, include your device model, operating-system version, and Gainer version when contacting support.

Weight units

Gainer supports kilograms and pounds. Changing the weight-unit setting converts stored workout values rather than changing only the unit label.

Android media controls

On supported Android devices, Gainer can display controls for the active media session during a workout. Availability varies by device and media app, and Android may require notification-listener or media access to be enabled. Some media apps may not support every control consistently.

iPhone media controls

The current iPhone beta does not include the Android-style media-control integration. This does not affect workout logging, workout history, or active-workout persistence.

Apple Health and Health Connect

The current Gainer version does not yet write workouts to Apple Health or Android Health Connect. Those integrations are planned separately and are not required for the current beta.

Medical and fitness use

Gainer is a workout-recording tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or individualized exercise prescriptions. Use appropriate judgment and consult a qualified professional when needed.
